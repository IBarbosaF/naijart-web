Join Naijart

We are looking for artists who want to show their work in Europe and for people willing to collaborate with the association. If that sounds like you, this is your place.

What you get by joining

International exposure

Your work reaches a European audience through our exhibitions, events and digital channels.

Community

You become part of a network of Nigerian artists and collaborators united by a shared purpose.

Resources and training

Access to workshops, mentoring and resources designed to boost your artistic career or your work as a collaborator.

Network of contacts

Direct connections with galleries, event organisers and other cultural associations in Europe.

How it works

  1. 01

    Send your application

    Fill in the form with your details and tell us a little about yourself.

  2. 02

    We review your profile

    Our team reviews every application carefully, with no rush and no automatic filters.

  3. 03

    We get in touch

    We email you to explain the next steps.

  4. 04

    You become part of Naijart

    We welcome you into the community and we start building together.
